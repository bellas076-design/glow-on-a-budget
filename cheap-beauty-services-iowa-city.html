What “cheap” means in Iowa City

Cheap shouldn’t mean careless. A low price is only a good deal if the work lasts and the salon keeps its tools clean. Every pick below has a Glow Score of 4.0 or higher, a visible state license and a standard of service that still feels worth it for a student budget.

Iowa City has three main kinds of budget options. Beauty school clinics charge the least because supervised students do the work. Independent studios downtown offer more polished spaces for a little more. Off-campus or student-friendly spots can still be better value than trendy downtown pricing.

Quick explainers

Haircuts

A basic cut includes a wash, the cut and a quick style. Color and blowouts cost extra.

Nails

A regular manicure lasts about a week. Gel lasts two to three weeks and often wins on value.

Waxing

Hot or soft wax removes hair from the root, and results last three to five weeks.

Brow lamination

A setting solution brushes brows upward for six to eight weeks.

Lash extensions

Individual lashes are glued to each natural lash. Count the fill price, not just the first set.

Spray tans

Booth tans are cheapest; airbrush sprays are usually more even and natural-looking.

How to avoid overpaying

  • Ask for the full price before you sit down. Gel removal, nail art and lash mapping fees can add $10 to $25.
  • Count the upkeep. A $79 lash set with $55 fills costs more over a semester than a $95 set with $45 fills.
  • Skip packages you won’t use. Prepaid bundles only save money if you’ll be in town to use them.
  • Check the license. Iowa requires cosmetology and esthetics licenses to be displayed.
