Cheap beauty services in Iowa City

Here are the lowest prices we found in Iowa City for every service students ask us about, from $14 haircuts to $8 brow waxes, and only at places that passed our cleanliness check.

Prices checked by Jordan Okafor in October 2026. Distances are walking or driving miles from the Pentacrest.

What "cheap" means in Iowa City

Cheap shouldn't mean careless. A low price is only a good deal if the work lasts and the salon keeps its tools clean. Every pick below has a Glow Score of 4.0 or higher, a visible state license and a clean result from our own visit.

Iowa City has three main kinds of budget options. Beauty school clinics charge the least because supervised students do the work. Independent studios downtown and on the Northside run student discounts to fill quiet weekday hours. Coralville shops near the mall are often cheaper but are a bus ride or a short drive from campus.

Quick explainers

Haircuts

A basic cut includes a wash, the cut and a quick style. Color and blowouts cost extra. If you only need a trim, say so when booking; several salons charge less for a dry trim.

Nails

A regular manicure lasts about a week. Gel is cured under a lamp and lasts two to three weeks, which usually makes it the better value even though it costs more upfront.

Waxing

Hot or soft wax removes hair from the root, and results last three to five weeks. Avoid retinol creams for a few days before a face wax to protect your skin.

Brow lamination

A setting solution brushes your brows into a fuller, upward shape that lasts six to eight weeks. It's a cheaper, lower-commitment option than microblading.

Lash extensions

Individual lashes are glued to your natural ones. They need a fill every two to three weeks, so count the fill price, not just the first set. See our lash guide.

Spray tans

A sunless solution darkens the top layer of skin for five to ten days. Booth tans are cheapest, and airbrush tans give a more even result. See our tan guide.

Best student discounts in town

  • Polished Pentacrest Nail Bar: $5 off any gel service on Tuesdays, and a free nail art accent after five visits.
  • Clipper & Comb Barbers: $18 cuts Monday through Wednesday for anyone with a university ID.
  • Lash Loft on Linn: 15% off full sets Monday through Thursday with a student ID.
  • Sunlit Studio Downtown: $28 airbrush tans Sunday through Wednesday instead of $35.
  • Smooth Studio Northside: a $40 "semester pass" that covers four brow waxes in one semester.
Save even more: Book the first slot of the day. Several studios told us they will add a small extra, like a free brow tint or a lash bath, for students who take the 9 a.m. appointment nobody else wants.

Walk-in friendly spots

Last-minute plans happen. These places reliably took walk-ins during our test visits on weekday afternoons:

  • Clipper & Comb Barbers, downtown: average wait of 15 minutes.
  • Polished Pentacrest Nail Bar, downtown: average wait of 20 minutes, longer on Fridays.
  • Sunlit Studio Downtown: same-day airbrush tans if you call before noon.
  • Glowhaus Tanning, southeast Iowa City: booth tans with almost no wait.

How to avoid overpaying

  1. Ask for the full price before you sit down. Gel removal, nail art and lash "mapping" fees can add $10 to $25.
  2. Count the upkeep. A $79 lash set with $55 fills costs more over a semester than a $95 set with $45 fills.
  3. Skip packages you won't use. Prepaid bundles only save money if you are in town to use them. Remember winter break.
  4. Check the license. Iowa requires cosmetology and esthetics licenses to be displayed. If you can't see one, ask.
