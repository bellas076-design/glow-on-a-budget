Cheap nails in Iowa City

Cheap nails from Iowa City nail salons that take walk-ins between classes. We compared five nail salons on gel and regular manicure prices, student deals, walk-in waits and how far they are from the Pentacrest.

Prices checked by Jordan Okafor in November 2026. Prices are for a basic manicure before tip unless noted.

Gel manicure price in Iowa City

A gel manicure price in Iowa City runs from $25 on a student deal day to about $50 at a high-end studio. A regular polish manicure costs $18 to $28. Gel costs more up front, but it is cured under a lamp and stays glossy for two to three weeks, while regular polish usually chips within a week.

Regular polish

Classic nail polish that air-dries. The cheapest option, easy to remove at home, and a good choice if you like changing colors every few days.

Gel manicure

Gel polish cured under an LED lamp. Dry the moment you leave and lasts two to three weeks. Needs a proper soak-off to remove.

Dip powder

Colored powder sealed onto the nail. Lasts up to four weeks and is very strong, but usually costs $5 to $10 more than gel.

Pedicure

A basic pedicure runs $35 to $55 in Iowa City. Most salons add gel polish to a pedicure for about $10 more.

Do the semester math: A 15-week semester of gel nails means about five sets, one every three weeks. At Polished Pentacrest on Tuesdays that is $125 plus tips. Regular polish redone every week at $18 adds up to $270 plus tips, so gel is the cheaper habit if you want your nails done all semester.

How to make a gel manicure last

  • Rub cuticle oil into your nails every night. Dry cuticles lift the gel at the edges.
  • Wear gloves for dishes and cleaning. Hot water and soap weaken gel faster than anything else.
  • Use your fingertips, not your nails, to open cans and peel stickers.
  • Never peel gel off. It takes layers of your natural nail with it, and repairs cost more than a removal.
  • Book a proper soak-off when you are done. Most salons charge $5 to $10, and Clinton Street Polish Bar does it free for students.

Signs of a clean nail salon

  • Metal tools come out of a sealed pouch or a sterilizer, not a drawer.
  • Files and buffers are new for each client or thrown away after use.
  • Pedicure tubs have disposable liners or are scrubbed between clients.
  • Each technician's Iowa license is on display. If you cannot see one, ask.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest nail salon near the University of Iowa?
Polished Pentacrest Nail Bar, with a $25 gel manicure on Tuesdays and an $18 regular manicure any day. It is about a two-minute walk from the Pentacrest.
Can I walk into a nail salon in Iowa City without an appointment?
Yes, at Polished Pentacrest Nail Bar on weekdays and at Coral Ridge Beauty Bar before 3 p.m. Downtown salons get busy on Fridays and before formals, so call ahead those days.
How much should I tip for a manicure?
Between 15 and 20 percent of the full price is standard. On a $30 gel manicure, that is about $5 to $6, even if you paid the student price.
How long does a gel manicure take?
About 45 minutes to an hour, or a little longer if old gel has to be removed first.
