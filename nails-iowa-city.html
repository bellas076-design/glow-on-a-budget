Cheap nails in Iowa City

Cheap nails from Iowa City nail salons that take walk-ins between classes. We compared five nail salons on gel and regular manicure prices, student deals, walk-in waits and how far they are from the Pentacrest.

Prices checked by Jordan Okafor in November 2026. Prices are for a basic manicure before tip unless noted.

Gel manicure price in Iowa City

A gel manicure price in Iowa City runs from $25 on a student deal day to about $50 at a high-end studio. A regular polish manicure costs $18 to $28. Gel costs more up front, but it stays glossy and durable for two to three weeks.

What to choose

Regular polish

Classic and inexpensive, but it usually chips within a week.

Gel manicure

Dry the moment you leave and lasts two to three weeks.

Dip powder

Strong and long-lasting, but often $5 to $10 more than gel.

Pedicure

A basic pedicure runs $35 to $55; a gel top coat often adds another $10.

How to make a gel manicure last

  • Rub cuticle oil into your nails every night.
  • Wear gloves for dishes and cleaning.
  • Use your fingertips, not your nails, to open cans or peel stickers.
  • Never peel gel off. It strips layers of your natural nail.
  • Book a proper soak-off when you are done; most salons charge $5 to $10.

Signs of a clean nail salon

  • Metal tools come out of a sealed pouch or sterilizer, not a drawer.
  • Files and buffers are new for each client or discarded after use.
  • Pedicure tubs have disposable liners or are scrubbed between clients.
  • Each technician’s Iowa license is on display.

Can I walk into a nail salon in Iowa City without an appointment?

How much should I tip for a manicure?
