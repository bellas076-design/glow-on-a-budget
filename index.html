Why a local guide?

The big beauty websites are great for reading about a new mascara, but they cannot tell you which lash studio on Linn Street gives a student discount on Tuesdays, or which tanning spot takes walk-ins the Friday before a formal. Their advice is written for the whole country. Ours is written for one college town, by people who live on a student budget.

Every listing on this site was visited, called or booked by a member of our team. We write down what we actually paid, how long we waited and whether the place was clean, then we compare it against everywhere else in town.

How we rate salons: the Glow Score

Every salon gets a Glow Score out of 5. It is not a popularity contest. We weigh the things students told us matter most when we surveyed 212 University of Iowa students in September 2026.

  • Price (35%): what a standard service costs before tip, compared with the Iowa City average.
  • Reputation (25%): how consistent the reviews are and whether our own visit matched them.
  • Cleanliness and licensing (20%): visible state licenses, clean tools and fresh supplies for each client.
  • Student perks (10%): discounts for a student ID, loyalty cards and group deals.
  • Convenience (10%): walk-in availability, evening hours and distance from the Pentacrest or a Cambus stop.

No salon pays to be listed, and no salon can pay to improve its score.

Quick tip: Ask every salon, "Do you have a student price?" before you book. In our survey, 41% of students had never asked, and nearly half of the salons we contacted offer something they do not advertise.

The $60-a-month glow plan

Our team built a sample monthly budget for a student who wants to look polished without spending more than a night out. Here is what $60 covers in Iowa City if you use the cheapest reputable options in our guides:

Questions we hear a lot

Is it safe to book at a beauty school clinic?
Yes, in our experience. Students at an esthetics or cosmetology academy work under licensed instructors who check their work. Services take longer, but prices are often half of what a salon charges.
Do I need to tip if I used a student discount?
Yes. Tip on the original price if you can, usually 15 to 20 percent. Your stylist's pay doesn't shrink just because the bill did.
How often do you update prices?
We confirm every price at the start of each semester and again before big event seasons like homecoming, formals and spring break.
