Why a local guide?

The big beauty websites are great for reading about new products, but they can’t tell you which Iowa City salon gives a student discount on Tuesdays or which tanning spot takes walk-ins between classes. This guide compares the places students actually use, with price, cleanliness, convenience and value in mind.

How we rate salons

  • Price: what the standard service costs compared to the local average.
  • Reputation: consistency, reviews and whether the experience matched the price.
  • Cleanliness and licensing: visible licenses, sanitized tools and a hygienic space.
  • Student perks: discounts, student days and loyalty savings.
  • Convenience: walk-in availability, distance and scheduling ease.

Questions we hear a lot

Is it safe to book at a beauty school clinic?
Yes. Students often work under licensed instructors, and they’re usually the best value if you’re comfortable with a longer appointment.
Do I need to tip if I used a student discount?
Usually yes. Tips are still expected on the full service price, even when the booking came with a discount.
How often do you update prices?
We check and refresh at the start of each semester and before big event seasons like formals, homecoming and spring break.
