Why a local guide?

The big beauty websites are great for reading about a new mascara, but they cannot tell you which lash studio on Linn Street gives a student discount on Tuesdays, or which tanning spot takes walk-ins before class.

Every listing on this site was visited, called or booked by a member of our team. We write down what we actually paid, how long we waited and whether the place was clean, then we compare it against nearby options so you know what is actually worth your money.
