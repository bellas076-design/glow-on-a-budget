What is a spray tan?

A spray tan uses a solution containing DHA, a sugar-based ingredient that reacts with the top layer of your skin to make it darker. It doesn’t involve UV light, so it skips the sun damage of a tanning bed while still giving a healthy glow.

Choose your finish

Airbrush tan

A technician sprays you by hand and adjusts the shade and coverage for a more natural, even result.

Booth tan

You stand in an automated booth that sprays you in a few passes. Fast and lowest cost.

Mobile tan

An airbrush technician brings a portable tent to your home, which is convenient for groups before formals.

How to prep

  • Exfoliate the day before so the tan goes on evenly.
  • Shave or wax at least 24 hours before your appointment.
  • Arrive with clean skin and skip lotion, deodorant, perfume and makeup.
  • Wear loose, dark clothes and slip-on shoes home.

Aftercare that makes it last

  • Wait 8 to 12 hours before your first shower.
  • Pat dry instead of rubbing with a towel.
  • Moisturize twice a day with an oil-free lotion.
  • Avoid long hot showers or the pool for the first few days.

Frequently asked questions

Will I turn orange?
Not if the technician matches the solution to your skin tone. Ask for a light shade on your first visit.
Is a spray tan safer than a tanning bed?
Yes. Spray tans do not use UV light. You still need sunscreen outside because they do not protect your skin from the sun.
When should I book before spring break?
Two to three weeks ahead. That is when every place on this list told us they get busiest.
