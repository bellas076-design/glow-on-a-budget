Spray tans in Iowa City

Iowa winters are long, and formal season comes fast. We compared five spray tan options around Iowa City, from $18 booth tans to a mobile tanner who comes to your apartment, so you can get a sun-free glow without overpaying.

Prices checked by Jordan Okafor in October 2026. Prices are for one full-body tan before tip.

What is a spray tan?

A spray tan uses a solution containing DHA, a sugar-based ingredient that reacts with the top layer of your skin to make it darker. It doesn't involve UV light, so it skips the sun damage of tanning beds. The color develops over 8 to 12 hours and fades in five to ten days as your skin naturally sheds.

Airbrush tan

A technician sprays you by hand and adjusts the shade and coverage as they go. The most natural, even result. Takes about 15 minutes.

Booth tan

You stand in an automated booth that sprays you in a few passes. Cheaper and faster, but it's easier to end up with streaks on hands and ankles.

Mobile tan

An airbrush technician brings a portable tent to your home. A bit more expensive alone, but group rates make it the best deal before formals.

Timing tip: Book your tan one to two days before your event, not the same day. That gives the color time to fully develop and gives you a chance to rinse off the extra bronzer before you put on a light-colored outfit.

How to prep

  • Exfoliate the day before so the tan goes on evenly and fades evenly.
  • Shave or wax at least 24 hours before your appointment, not the same day.
  • Arrive with clean skin. Skip lotion, deodorant, perfume and makeup.
  • Wear loose, dark clothes and slip-on shoes to wear home.

Aftercare that makes it last

  • Wait 8 to 12 hours before your first shower, and rinse with lukewarm water only.
  • Pat dry instead of rubbing with a towel.
  • Moisturize twice a day with an oil-free lotion.
  • Avoid long hot showers, hot tubs and the Field House pool for the first few days.

Frequently asked questions

Will I turn orange?
Not if the technician matches the solution to your skin tone. Ask for a lighter shade on your first visit. You can always go darker next time.
Is a spray tan safer than a tanning bed?
Yes. Spray tans don't use UV light. You still need sunscreen outside, because a spray tan doesn't protect your skin from the sun.
When should I book before spring break?
Two to three weeks ahead. Every place on this list told us the week before spring break is their busiest of the year.
