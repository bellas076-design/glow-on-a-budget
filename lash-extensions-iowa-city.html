Lash extensions in Iowa City

Lash extensions are one of the easiest beauty services to overpay for. We compared five Iowa City studios on full-set price, fill price, student discounts and how quickly you can get an appointment.

Prices checked by Maya Thornquist in October 2026. Prices are for a classic full set before tip unless noted.

What are lash extensions?

A lash artist uses medical-grade adhesive to attach a single synthetic lash to each of your natural lashes, one at a time. A first full set takes about two hours. Because your natural lashes continue to shed, you will need fills every two to three weeks to keep the look full.

What to choose

Classic

One extension per natural lash. It looks like great mascara and is the most budget-friendly style.

Hybrid

A mix of single lashes and small fans. Fuller and textured, usually $20 to $30 more than classic.

Volume

Several ultra-light lashes fan onto each natural lash for dramatic results. It is the most expensive and time-intensive.

Lash lift

No extensions at all. Your natural lashes are curled and set for six to eight weeks.

Aftercare that makes your set last

  • Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours.
  • Use an oil-free cleanser around your eyes.
  • Brush them gently each morning with a clean spoolie.
  • Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase if possible.
  • Never pull loose extensions; let them fall out naturally or have them professionally removed.

Red flags to walk away from

  • No esthetics license on display.
  • Reusing tweezers without cleaning them in between clients.
  • Clusters of lashes glued across several natural lashes at once.
  • Burning or stinging that doesn’t stop shortly after application.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get lashes the same day in Iowa City?
Sometimes. Coral Ridge Beauty Bar accepts weekday walk-ins before 3 p.m. and Northside Lash Co. posts same-day cancellations.
How much should I tip my lash artist?
Between 15 and 20 percent of the full price is standard. On a $95 set, that’s about $15 to $19.
Will extensions ruin my natural lashes?
Not when they’re applied correctly and removed by a professional. Damage usually comes from overly heavy sets or pulling them off at home.
