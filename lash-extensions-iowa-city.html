Lash extensions in Iowa City

Lash extensions are one of the easiest beauty services to overpay for. We compared five Iowa City studios on full-set price, fill price, student discounts and how quickly you can get an appointment.

Prices checked by Maya Thornquist in October 2026. Prices are for a classic full set before tip unless noted.

What are lash extensions?

A lash artist uses medical-grade adhesive to attach a single synthetic lash to each of your natural lashes, one at a time. A first full set takes about two hours. Because your natural lashes shed every few weeks, the extensions fall out with them, so most people come back for a "fill" every two to three weeks to replace what's missing.

Classic

One extension per natural lash. Looks like great mascara. The cheapest style and the best place to start.

Hybrid

A mix of single lashes and small fans. Fuller and a little textured. Usually $20 to $30 more than classic.

Volume

Several ultra-light lashes fanned onto each natural lash for a dramatic look. The most expensive and the most time in the chair.

Lash lift (the budget swap)

No extensions at all. Your natural lashes are curled and set for six to eight weeks. A good choice if you can't afford regular fills.

Our picks

$110 set, $45 fills for students

The most consistent work we saw. The $10 student fill discount means it gets cheaper the longer you stay, which matters more than the first-set price.

A five-minute walk from class, with a weekday student price that beats most of downtown. Book early in the week for the discount.

$55 set, $30 fills

Supervised student artists, so plan for three hours instead of two. Our tester's set lasted nearly as long as a salon set.

Do the semester math: If you keep lashes for a full 15-week semester, you'll need one full set and about five fills. That's roughly $305 at Lash Loft on Linn with the student discount, $335 at Northside Lash Co. with the fill discount, and $205 at the academy.

Aftercare that makes your set last

  • Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours. Skip the gym and the steam shower that day.
  • Use an oil-free cleanser around your eyes. Oil breaks down lash glue.
  • Brush them gently each morning with a clean spoolie.
  • Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase if you can.
  • Never pull at loose extensions. Let them fall out on their own or have your artist remove them.

Red flags to walk away from

  • No state esthetics license on display.
  • The artist reuses tweezers without cleaning them between clients.
  • Clusters of lashes glued across several natural lashes at once. This can damage your natural lashes.
  • Stinging or burning that doesn't stop after a few minutes. Ask to stop and have the area rinsed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get lashes the same day in Iowa City?
Sometimes. Coral Ridge Beauty Bar takes weekday walk-ins before 3 p.m., and Northside Lash Co. posts same-day cancellations on its booking page. Downtown studios are usually booked a few days out, especially before formals.
How much should I tip my lash artist?
Between 15 and 20 percent of the full price is standard. On a $95 set, that's about $15 to $19.
Will extensions ruin my natural lashes?
Not when they're applied correctly and removed by a professional. Damage usually comes from extensions that are too heavy or from pulling them off at home.
